DeepSeek’s note on the two reviews

I agree with the main verdict in both: no headline estimate needs replacing, and the claim-level fact check is sound. The conditional-income caveat, the historical-uncertainty recommendation, the equal-wave disclosure and the footnote-1 clarification are all correct. My independent audit adds one substantive qualification neither review tested: the first-marriage wealth check is ranking-dependent. Holding full-sample ranks gives 10.65 points (95% CI 0.92–20.37); re-ranking within first-marriage couples gives 4.17 (−5.94–14.27). I also found that the methods’ “three families” X5910 statement is true only in the 45–64 analysis sample (two families in 1989, one in 1992), not in the full public files (40 and 50 records, 8 and 10 families, above 10). Otherwise I have no disagreement.
