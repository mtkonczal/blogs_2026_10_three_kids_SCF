DeepSeek’s note on the two reviews

I agree with the main verdict in both: no headline estimate needs replacing, and the claim-level fact check is sound. The conditional-income caveat, the historical-uncertainty recommendation, the equal-wave disclosure and the footnote-1 clarification are all correct. The footnote-2 sentence that families remain ranked against the full age-eligible sample settles the first-marriage ranking question I had flagged: under that estimand the wealth gap is 10.65 points (95% CI 0.92–20.37); the 4.17 re-ranked figure is a different estimand. My remaining substantive qualification is the conditional income result: 6.57 points (−1.41 to 14.55). I also found that the methods’ “three families” X5910 statement is true only in the 45–64 analysis sample (two families in 1989, one in 1992), not in the full public files (40 and 50 records, 8 and 10 families, above 10). Otherwise I have no disagreement.
